- **2026-07-16 (pm11)** — **Two direct bug reports from the founder, both fixed.** Not audit-derived — the founder came back from testing with two concrete complaints. **(1) Reschedule-week vs. split order:** "it made today pull day instead of push day and moved push to saturday." Traced to `lib/weekReschedule.ts`'s `planWeekReschedule`: each workout independently searches for its OWN best-scoring day (by muscle-recovery penalty via `scoreDay`), with no concept that workouts belong to a deliberate sequence — so a later workout (Pull) can grab an earlier day than one that was scheduled ahead of it (Push) purely because Pull's regions happened to score marginally better there.
