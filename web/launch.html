Inside Arclo

Four things, done properly.

The schedule

A real time slot Arclo protects.

Connect Google or Apple Calendar once. Arclo finds your free windows, places each session to fit your day and recovery, and writes it back to your calendar.

  • Gap-aware placement. Sessions land in windows long enough to actually finish them.
  • Conflict detection. A meeting lands on your workout and Arclo tells you, or on Pro, just moves it.
  • Availability you control. Mark days off, set earliest and latest times, switch on Travel Mode.
  • Reminders that make sense. A nudge before the session you scheduled, not at a random 6 PM.
Calendar view
The session

A logbook that already knows your numbers.

Open a session and the weights are already filled in, picked from what you lifted last time and how it felt. Log a set with one tap and keep moving.

  • Today’s target, pre-filled. Progressive overload applied automatically, set by set.
  • 1,300+ exercises with form guides, plus swap-any-exercise for the gear you actually have.
  • Works offline. Every set saves locally, even with no signal, and syncs when you’re back.
  • Was that too hard? One answer at the end reshapes next week’s loads.
Live workout
The adaptation

It reads the week you actually had.

Arclo runs a real training block: three weeks of building, then a lighter deload week. When your logs say the block isn’t landing, the block changes, not you.

  • Missed a few? Arclo drops into a recovery block and re-stamps every upcoming session, not just today’s.
  • Crushing it? Say a session was too easy enough times and Arclo gives you harder exercises and bigger jumps.
  • Scheduled deloads. Week four backs off on purpose, the way a coach would program it.
  • Away from your gym? Travel Mode rewrites upcoming sessions for whatever equipment you have.
Session feedback
The proof

Consistency you can actually see.

Not vanity charts. A completion rate that tells the truth, a streak that survives a bad week, and per-lift strength curves that show whether the last month did anything.

  • Consistency score: the share of scheduled sessions you actually completed.
  • Volume & strength trends per lift, with every personal record kept.
  • Weight & measurement history with a real trend line, not just today’s number.
  • Weekly recap and badges: a short read on Sunday, not a slot machine.
